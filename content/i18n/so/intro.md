## Ku saabsan

**ArchLingmo** waa nooc bulsho oo [Lingmo OS](https://lingmo.org) ah oo loogu talagalay Arch Linux,
kaas oo desktop-ka oo dhan loo raray **Qt 6**, isbeddelladana waxaa loo dirayaa mashruuca rasmiga ah.

- **Muuqaal qurux badan**: bar-ka sare, dock-ga, Spotlight, kontaroollada iyo badhamada daaqadaha oo dib loo naqshadeeyay, mawduuc iftiin iyo mid mugdi ah.
- **Casri ah**: Qt 6, KDE Frameworks 6 iyo KWin 6, si toos ah uga yimid kaydadka Arch.
- **Waxtar leh**: gaaban-furayaal leh furaha Super, bogag gaaban-furayaasha iyo bilowga ah oo ku jira Dejinta, iyo saxanka nidaamka oo leh abka aad caadiga u isticmaasho.
- **Furan**: koodhka oo dhan wuxuu ku jiraa GitHub, horumar kastana wuxuu noqdaa pull request loo diro Lingmo.
