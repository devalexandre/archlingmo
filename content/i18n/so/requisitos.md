## Shuruudaha ugu yar

| | Ugu yaraan | La soo jeediyay |
|---|---|---|
| **Processor** | x86-64 (64 bit) | 4 core |
| **Xusuusta (RAM)** | 4 GB | 8 GB ama ka badan |
| **Disk** | 20 GB | 40 GB, SSD |
| **Garaafyada** | OpenGL 2.1 (Intel, AMD, NVIDIA) | Kaarka garaafyada oo leh darawal cusub |
| **Bilowga** | BIOS ama UEFI | UEFI |

Mashiinnada farsamada (VirtualBox, VMware, GNOME Boxes, virt-manager), u qoondee ugu yaraan
**4 GB oo xusuus ah** oo daar **dardargelinta 3D**.
