## Fepetra farafahakeliny

| | Farafahakeliny | Atoro |
|---|---|---|
| **Processeur** | x86-64 (64 bits) | Core 4 |
| **Fitadidiana** | 4 GB | 8 GB na mihoatra |
| **Kapila** | 20 GB | 40 GB, SSD |
| **Sary (GPU)** | OpenGL 2.1 (Intel, AMD, NVIDIA) | Karatra misy driver vaovao |
| **Fanombohana** | BIOS na UEFI | UEFI |

Ho an'ny milina virtoaly (VirtualBox, VMware, GNOME Boxes, virt-manager), omeo farafahakeliny
**fitadidiana 4 GB** ary alefaso ny **fanafainganana 3D**.
