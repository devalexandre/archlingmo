## Mahitaji ya chini

| | Kiwango cha chini | Kinachopendekezwa |
|---|---|---|
| **Prosesa** | x86-64 (biti 64) | Kori 4 |
| **Kumbukumbu** | GB 4 | GB 8 au zaidi |
| **Diski** | GB 20 | GB 40, SSD |
| **Michoro** | OpenGL 2.1 (Intel, AMD, NVIDIA) | Kadi yenye kiendeshi kilichosasishwa |
| **Uwashaji** | BIOS au UEFI | UEFI |

Kwa mashine pepe (VirtualBox, VMware, GNOME Boxes, virt-manager), tenga angalau
**GB 4 za kumbukumbu** na uwashe **uharakishaji wa 3D**.
