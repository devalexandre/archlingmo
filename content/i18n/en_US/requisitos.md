## Minimum requirements

| | Minimum | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| **Processor** | x86-64 (64-bit) | 4 cores |
| **Memory** | 4 GB | 8 GB or more |
| **Disk** | 20 GB | 40 GB, SSD |
| **Graphics** | OpenGL 2.1 (Intel, AMD, NVIDIA) | Card with an up-to-date driver |
| **Boot** | BIOS or UEFI | UEFI |

For virtual machines (VirtualBox, VMware, GNOME Boxes, virt-manager), give it at least
**4 GB of memory** and turn on **3D acceleration**.
