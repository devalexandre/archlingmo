## About

**ArchLingmo** is a community edition of [Lingmo OS](https://lingmo.org) for Arch Linux,
with the whole desktop ported to **Qt 6** while the changes are sent upstream to the official project.

- **Polished look**: redesigned top bar, dock, Spotlight, controls and window buttons, in light and dark themes.
- **Up to date**: Qt 6, KDE Frameworks 6 and KWin 6, straight from the Arch repositories.
- **Practical**: Super key shortcuts, Shortcuts and Startup pages in Settings, and a system tray with your everyday apps.
- **Open**: all the code is on GitHub, and every improvement becomes a pull request to Lingmo.
