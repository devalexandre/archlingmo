## Exigenties minimal

| | Minimal | Recomandat |
|---|---|---|
| **Processor** | x86-64 (64 bits) | 4 nucleos |
| **Memorie** | 4 GB | 8 GB o plu |
| **Disco** | 20 GB | 40 GB, SSD |
| **Grafica** | OpenGL 2.1 (Intel, AMD, NVIDIA) | Carte con un actual driver |
| **Inicialisation** | BIOS o UEFI | UEFI |

Por machines virtual (VirtualBox, VMware, GNOME Boxes, virt-manager), reservar adminim
**4 GB de memorie** e activar li **acceleration 3D**.
